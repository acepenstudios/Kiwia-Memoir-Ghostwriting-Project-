01 / The first conversation

A straightforward conversation about a life, in the subject’s own words.

The proposed book is intended to document your father's life in a clear chronological account. The discovery consultation is an opportunity to hear directly from him, understand the broad shape of his story and discuss what he hopes the finished book will achieve.

The conversation is exploratory. It is not a full interview series, a manuscript draft or the start of the complete ghostwriting assignment.

Who should attend?

The Interview Subject should be present.

The person whose life is to be documented is expected to participate personally in the discovery consultation. Egejn is welcome to join the conversation as the commissioning client.

This allows the project to begin with the Interview Subject’s own account, preferences and expectations, rather than relying only on a third-party description of his life.

03 / What happens next

A considered process, with a defined scope at each stage.

01

Discovery consultation

A one-hour conversation to understand the Interview Subject’s story, the intended book and the broad requirements of the project.

02

Project assessment and proposal

If commissioned, Ace Pen Studio develops a project-specific proposal covering the writing approach, preliminary structure, scope, deliverables, schedule and responsibilities.

03

Engagement agreement

Following proposal approval and payment of the outstanding proposal fee, a Client Engagement Agreement is prepared. Ghostwriting begins only after the agreement is executed and the agreed initial payment requirements are met.
