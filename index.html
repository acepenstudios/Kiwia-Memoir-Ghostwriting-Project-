Welcome

Thank you for considering Ace Pen Studio to document your father's life story.

Based on your initial enquiry, the proposed direction is a chronological life-story memoir: a clear, engaging account tracing his journey from his early years through the experiences and milestones that have shaped his life.

Ace Pen Studio proposes a hybrid interview-and-transcription approach, supported by editorial development and professional ghostwriting. This page explains the discovery process, consultation arrangements, professional fees and the steps towards commissioning the book.

02 /

The discovery consultation

The first stage is a one-hour conversation with the ghostwriter. Its purpose is to understand the Interview Subject, the motivation behind the book and the intended outcome of the project.

Background & early life

  • Where was he born and raised?
  • What was his family background and childhood like?
  • What shaped his formative years?

Education & development

  • What was his educational journey?
  • Who and what influenced him?
  • Which decisions shaped his life?

Career & life experiences

  • What has his professional and personal journey looked like?
  • What were his achievements and challenges?
  • What were the major turning points?

Family & personal life

  • What role has family played in his life?
  • Which relationships matter to his story?
  • What personal experiences should be documented?

Reflections & legacy

  • What does he consider his greatest accomplishments?
  • What lessons has life taught him?
  • What should future generations remember?

The book

  • What should the book achieve?
  • Who is its intended readership?
  • What level of detail is envisaged?
  • Are photographs or documents available?
Why we hold this conversation

The discovery conversation will form part of the ghostwriter's working reference materials. It will help establish the narrative direction, identify important periods in the Interview Subject's life and guide subsequent interviews and manuscript development. It is a preliminary discovery exercise, not the commencement of the full ghostwriting assignment.

Participation and recording

The Interview Subject — your father, whose life is to be documented — is expected to participate personally in the discovery consultation. You are welcome to join the conversation.

The consultation is intended to be audio-recorded for transcription and professional reference purposes. The recording will only begin after the Interview Subject has received an explanation and given his own informed consent. If he does not consent, the consultation can proceed without recording, with notes taken only if he agrees.

Appointment confirmation

All appointments are subject to confirmation by Ace Pen Studio. Once the selected slot is confirmed, the studio will send a calendar invitation and the relevant consultation invoice. A requested slot is not booked until APS confirms it.

Project assessment & proposal development

Following the discovery consultation, Ace Pen Studio will assess the project and prepare a formal proposal setting out the recommended editorial direction, ghostwriting methodology, anticipated manuscript structure, scope, deliverables, schedule, responsibilities and financial arrangements for the proposed ghostwriting assignment.

Separate professional fee · Project proposal
TZS 250,000

Initial instalment — TZS 125,000 (50%)
Payable when the proposal is commissioned and before development begins.

Final instalment — TZS 125,000 (50%)
Payable upon approval of the completed proposal.

The proposal fee covers professional assessment, planning and preparation. It is separate from the eventual ghostwriting fee and is not an advance payment towards manuscript writing.

What happens next

  1. Discovery consultationA one-hour conversation with the Interview Subject and commissioning client, subject to the consent arrangements described above.
  2. Project assessment and proposalOnce commissioned and the initial TZS 125,000 instalment is paid, APS develops the project proposal.
  3. Proposal review and approvalThe completed proposal is submitted for review. The remaining TZS 125,000 is payable upon approval.
  4. Client Engagement AgreementAfter proposal approval and settlement of the proposal fee, APS prepares an agreement covering the full ghostwriting fees, scope, deliverables, schedule, responsibilities, intellectual property and other terms.
  5. Project commencementThe ghostwriting assignment begins after the agreement is executed and the agreed commencement and payment requirements are met. January 2027 is the proposed window for initial project activities, subject to the approved schedule.
